Research

My research studies how large language models and recommender systems can benefit from each other. I am particularly interested in converting rich item semantics into representations that can be efficiently learned and served by industrial ranking systems.

LLM for Recommendation
Semantic item representations, generative recommendation, item tokenization.
Semantic ID
RQ-VAE, LLM embedding alignment, co-occurrence structure, geographic tokens.
Sequential & Cross-domain Rec.
ID-content alignment, contrastive learning, behavior sequence modeling.
LLM Evaluation
Hierarchical task trees, human alignment criteria, automatic evaluation reliability.
I write ongoing paper readings and research notes at RecSys & LLM Frontier Notes.
